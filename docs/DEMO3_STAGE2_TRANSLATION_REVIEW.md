# Demo 3 Stage 2 Translation Review

## Status

Forty-six Russian and forty-six English translation drafts were created from the exact
Uzbek candidate record IDs, versions, and content hashes. Every draft is unapproved,
inactive, quarantined, and runtime-ineligible. Approval of an Uzbek record does not
approve either translation.

## Terms requiring human language review

| Uzbek/source term | Draft handling | Required reviewer decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `BHM` | Russian uses `БРВ`; English uses `BCU` | Confirm RTMC's approved public abbreviation or spell it out |
| `JSHSHIR` | Russian adds `ПИНФЛ`; English retains `JSHSHIR` | Confirm legally preferred terminology per language |
| `Tizim operatori` | Rendered as UZIMEI/system operator by context | Confirm the official organization term |
| `resipient-operator` | Rendered as new/recipient operator | Confirm the approved MNP term |
| `bojxona kirim orderi` | Rendered as customs import order | Confirm the official customs translation |
| `biometrik harakatlanish hujjati` | Rendered as biometric travel document | Legal-language review required |
| `TAC` | Retained as TAC | Confirm whether an explanatory expansion is required |
| registration deadline vs tariff boundary | Kept explicitly separate | Do not translate the quarantined 30/60-day interpretation |
| blocked/unblocked service wording | Rendered conservatively | Confirm operator-specific terminology |

Language reviewers must compare every translation with its linked Uzbek record and
official evidence, record requested changes, and approve each language independently.
No translation may be described as official before that review.
