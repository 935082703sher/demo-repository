## Positions requiring reconciliation

Departmental FAQ position: residents are assigned 30 calendar days and nonresidents
60 calendar days after SIM-slot activation to register an IMEI.

Official tariff-page position: the current UZIMEI tariff page groups resident and
nonresident individuals together and distinguishes registration within 30 calendar
days from registration after 30 days, measured from the first network event:
<https://uzimei.uz/tariffs>.

These statements may address different concepts: a registration-eligibility deadline
and a tariff boundary. That possibility is not an interpretation or resolution. RTMC
must establish the governing rule and approve exact citizen-facing wording.

## Questions requiring written answers

1. Do nonresidents legally have 60 days to register an IMEI after the first network

2. If 60 days is correct, is this a registration eligibility deadline rather than a
   tariff boundary?
3. If registration occurs during days 31–60, which tariff applies?
4. Does the official UZIMEI tariff structure apply equally to residents and
   nonresidents?
5. What current legal act, clause, official instruction, or approved source
   establishes the answer?
6. What is the source's effective date?
7. Should the departmental FAQ be corrected?
8. Who is the named RTMC IMEI content owner approving the corrected wording?
9. Who is the named authorized legal reviewer approving the interpretation?
